```sql
SELECT DISTINCT TOP 50 T1.VendAccount, T1.VendName
FROM VendTable AS T1
INNER JOIN PurchTable AS T2
  ON T1.VendAccount = T2.OrderAccount

  T2.PurchStatus = 1 -- 1 typically represents 'Open order' status
  AND YEAR(T2.CreatedDateTime) = YEAR(GETDATE());
```

### Explanation

This query retrieves a distinct list of the top 50 vendor accounts and names (from VendTable) that have at least one purchase order (from PurchTable) with a status of 'Open order' (PurchStatus = 1) and was created in the current year.
